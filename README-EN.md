# Liang Wenfeng's Perspective & Public First-Hand Materials

> History never raises its voice. In 2025, when DeepSeek turned Silicon Valley upside down, Liang Wenfeng quietly returned to his hometown in Zhanjiang and played a football match with his junior high school classmates. In the same year, he was named one of Nature's 10 people who mattered in science, and the R1 paper became the first cover article of a mainstream large model to undergo peer review; a year later, he completed the largest single-round financing in the history of China's AI industry, contributing 20 billion yuan personally, while using a limited partnership structure to keep voting rights firmly in his hands. To the outside world, Liang Wenfeng always presents two faces: one is that of a researcher who wears the same clothes, skips team-building, and takes seriously only architecture finalization and the role of corresponding author on papers; the other is that of a strategist who treats "cost" as the first-principles variable, trades open source for ecosystem, trades restraint for legitimacy, and keeps his head down doing the math while everyone else chases the hype. As a phenomenal figure in the AI era, a leader in China's transition from "borrowing" to "independent innovation," and the "Liang Sheng" (Saint Liang) in the hearts of countless DeepSeek users worldwide, the thinking and vision Liang Wenfeng demonstrates are worth studying by everyone.
